Put your photos here, in the SAME "images" folder (no subfolders needed).

Named exactly:

  aww.jpeg          -> "Awww saloma is so sad… LETS CHANGE THAT FROWN INTO A SMILE"
  1.jpeg, 2.jpeg, 10.jpeg          -> "To more happy moments together"
  3.jpeg, 15.jpeg                 -> "for more sleepy lomy lomy salomy"
  5.jpeg, 17.jpeg, 18.jpeg, 19.jpeg -> "she is so pretty that her soul refuses to let go of her beauty"
  20.jpeg                         -> "My most gorgeous divorced dad"
  21.jpeg, 22.jpeg                -> "THE PRETTIEST TO EVER TOUCH THE LANDS OF SPAIN"
  23.jpeg                         -> "Literally me"
  last.jpeg         -> shown big on the final scene, after "One more thing…"

Any OTHER numbered photo (4.jpeg, 6.jpeg, 7.jpeg, 8.jpeg, 9.jpeg, 11-14.jpeg,
16.jpeg, 24.jpeg, 25.jpeg, ... up to 40.jpeg) is picked up automatically and
shown under "More randoms of zeko's best person" — you don't need to list
them anywhere, just name them "<number>.jpeg" and drop them in.

If you end up with more than 40 numbered photos, open script.js and raise
STORY.randomsMaxIndex (search for it near the top).

Missing files are handled gracefully — the site just skips any photo that
isn't here yet instead of showing a broken image icon, so it's safe to
deploy before every photo is uploaded.
